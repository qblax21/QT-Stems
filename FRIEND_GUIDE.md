# How to connect to QT Stems

QT Stems splits songs into vocals, drums, bass and the rest. It runs on my PC, so your songs and downloads live there, in your own private library that nobody else can see.

## 1. One-time setup (5 minutes)
1. Install **Tailscale** (free) from tailscale.com/download on the phone or computer you'll use.
2. Open the **invite link I sent you** and sign in to Tailscale with any Google, Microsoft or Apple account. Accept the invite. Tailscale should now say **Connected**.
3. Open this address in your browser:

   **https://YOUR-PC-NAME.YOUR-TAILNET.ts.net/qt-stems.html**

   On an iPhone, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen** to get an app icon.
4. Type the **passcode I sent you separately**. Keep it private; it is your login.

## 2. Using it
- **Split a song:** upload a file (up to 200 MB), wait for it to finish, then open it from your Library.
- You can have **2 songs waiting at a time**, and your library holds about **5 GB**. Delete old songs to free space.
- Splitting uses my PC's graphics card, so it can take a few minutes if it's busy.

## 3. If it doesn't work
1. Open Tailscale and make sure it says **Connected** (switch it off and on again if not).
2. Open the status page: **https://YOUR-PC-NAME.YOUR-TAILNET.ts.net/status**
   - Both lines say *up*: the problem is your passcode or your connection. Re-type the passcode carefully. After 5 wrong tries you're locked out for 10 minutes.
   - It won't load, or says *not reachable*: my PC is probably off or asleep. Message me.
3. Still stuck? Send me:
   - a **screenshot** of what you see (and of the status page),
   - what device you're on (iPhone, Android, Mac, Windows) and the time it happened,
   - what you tapped just before.

   **Never send me your passcode.** If you lose it or think someone saw it, tell me and I'll give you a new one.
